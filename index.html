How To Order

Getting your favourite Dodo dishes is easy. Here are three ways:

Order on WhatsApp:
Send us a message at 08032107954 with what you’d like and where you are. We’ll confirm your order and agree the delivery fee with you before you pay.

Pickup Coming soon

Pick up in Lekki:
We’re opening a takeaway and pickup spot in Lekki, open 9am–9pm daily. Order ahead on WhatsApp and grab your food on the way.

Order on Glovo:
Prefer an app? Click the “Order on Glovo” button below, choose your dishes and pay online. Your meal will be delivered straight to your door.
