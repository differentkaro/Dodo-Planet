Your privacy, in plain words

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Who we are

This website belongs to Dodo Planet Ltd (RC 6892497), a food business in Lagos, Nigeria. If you have any question about your information, email hello@dodoplanet.ng or message us on WhatsApp at 08032107954.

What this website collects

You don’t need an account to use this website, and we don’t use advertising trackers. The “Send us a message” box doesn’t save what you type: it simply opens WhatsApp with your message ready to send.

Analytics (only if you say yes). We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages they find useful. It uses cookies and collects information such as the pages you view, your approximate location (city level), and your device and browser type. It only switches on after you tap “Okay” on our cookie notice, and you can change your mind at any time: change my cookie choice. Google’s own privacy policy applies to this data.

Like every website, our hosting provider (Netlify) keeps basic technical logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, to keep the site running and secure.

When you order or message us

When you contact us on WhatsApp or by email, we receive what you share with us: usually your name, phone number, delivery address and your order. We use it only to prepare and deliver your food, answer your questions and, if you agree, tell you about new dishes or our Lekki opening.

We don’t sell your information, and we only share it with someone who needs it to get your order to you, such as a delivery rider.

Ordering through Glovo or WhatsApp

Orders placed on Glovo are handled by Glovo, and messages on WhatsApp are handled by WhatsApp (Meta). Their own privacy policies apply to the information you give them.

How long we keep it

We keep order details only as long as we need them to serve you and to meet our tax and legal duties, then we delete them.

Your rights

Under Nigeria’s Data Protection Act 2023, you can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask us to stop sending you updates at any time. Just email or WhatsApp us and we’ll sort it out.
